# Related Projects And Research

## Framing

This page is intentionally broader than an Ouroboros bibliography. It collects
the projects and research threads that most directly inform the bigger vision.

## Direct Lineage And Close Neighbors

- `razzant/ouroboros` and the `joi-lab` continuation for the clearest local
  lineage of the idea
- `joi-lab/ouroboros-desktop` for the hardened local-host successor
- `EvoAgentX/EvoAgentX` for explicit workflow generation, evaluation, and
  self-evolution in a framework form

## Concept Cousins

- `openclaw/openclaw` for a gateway-centric control plane with multi-surface
  attachment and policy-heavy runtime control
- `steveyegge/gastown` for durable orchestration above external agent runtimes
- `CharlesQ9/Self-Evolving-Agents` for a survey-style concept index around
  self-evolving agents
- essays on self-improving coding agents, where the emphasis is feedback loops,
  evaluation, and operational refinement rather than singular prompt cleverness

## Working Conclusion

The wider research picture suggests that the next stage after Ouroboros is not
merely a larger self-edit loop. It is a convergence of:

- workflow evolution
- lifelong memory
- topology-aware multi-agent design
- explicit control-plane governance
- stronger safety and recovery machinery

That convergence is what the rest of this folder is trying to map.
